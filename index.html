Introduction

What I work on

I study finance at the University of Victoria's Gustavson School of Business, graduating in 2027, and I sit CFA Level 1 in August 2026.

Most of my time goes to valuation work and case competitions. I've written equity research on Air Canada for the CFA Institute Research Challenge, built a €300M capital deployment model and a 10,000-path Monte Carlo pension simulation, and spent two summers consulting: process and cost work at SAMH, then operations and research at Xenex.

I use AI tools heavily in the analysis itself rather than around the edges of it, which is the part of my work that tends to surprise people.
