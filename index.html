White paper · September 2026

One analyst and ten agents

Over the summer of 2026 I wrote equity research on nine companies with help from an AI model, then built a second version of the process in which ten AI agents gather evidence, argue and rule on each call. I ran both on Microsoft, Coca-Cola and Alphabet.

They agreed on the first two. On Alphabet the agents called a SELL where I had a HOLD. The paper covers what I built, what each run cost, what went wrong, and what I'll check when the calls come due in June 2027.

Introduction

What I work on

I build AI agents and automation tools, mostly for finance work. The largest so far is a pipeline of ten agents that research a company, argue both sides and make a BUY, HOLD or SELL call. The white paper above compares it with research I wrote myself.

Finance is the subject I know best, which is why the tools point at it. I study at UVic Gustavson, graduating in 2027. I've written equity research on Air Canada for the CFA Institute Research Challenge, built a €300M capital deployment model and a 10,000-path Monte Carlo pension simulation, and spent two summers consulting at SAMH and Xenex.
