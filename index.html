About

Building where AI meets finance

I build AI agents and automation tools, and point them at the finance work I know best.

I'm a BCom student at UVic Gustavson, graduating in 2027. I grew up in Bangalore. Finishing high school there, I wanted to study abroad, and with my parents backing me I started at the University of Victoria in 2023. Outside finance I shoot landscape photography, play table tennis, and follow F1.

The largest thing I've built so far is a pipeline of ten agents that research a company, argue both sides and make a BUY, HOLD or SELL call. The white paper compares it with research I wrote myself.

Finance is the subject I know best, which is why the tools point at it. I've written equity research on Air Canada for the CFA Institute Research Challenge, built a €300M capital deployment model and a 10,000-path Monte Carlo pension simulation, and spent two summers consulting at SAMH and Xenex.
