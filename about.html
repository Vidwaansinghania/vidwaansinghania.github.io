About

Bangalore to Victoria

I study finance at UVic Gustavson, graduating in 2027, and I sit CFA Level 1 in August 2026.

Now

What I spend my time on

Most of my time goes to valuation work and case competitions. That means DCFs and relative valuation, capital deployment models, Monte Carlo simulation, and the kind of consulting work where you have to quantify the answer rather than assert it.

I use AI tools heavily inside the analysis rather than around the edges of it. I follow the field closely and I've put a lot of hours into learning where these tools are genuinely reliable and where they quietly aren't.

Outside finance I shoot landscape photography, play table tennis, and follow F1.

Background

How I got here

I grew up in Bangalore. Finishing high school there, I wanted to study abroad, and with my parents backing me I started at the University of Victoria in 2023.

Three years in, the plan has sharpened. I came for a business degree and found the parts I actually care about: valuation, capital allocation, and building the model that settles an argument. Along the way I co-founded the UVic Entrepreneurship Club, worked two consulting summers, and represented UVic at the CFA Institute Research Challenge.

I'm looking for finance and consulting roles for after I graduate in 2027.
